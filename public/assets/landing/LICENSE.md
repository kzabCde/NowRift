# NowRift cinematic landing art

SPDX-License-Identifier: CC0-1.0

`forest.webp` and `foreground.webp` are original image-generated pixel-fantasy
artwork created for NowRift. The editable original masters are retained in
`art/landing/`. No reference image pixels, third-party game sprites, text, or
logos were incorporated.

`haven.webp`, `story.webp` and `progression.webp` are crops of real NowRift browser
captures retained in `docs/images/pixel-art/`. `exploration.webp` is the existing
Lost Desert world-preview artwork. Their original local game art is CC0.

These images are provided under CC0 1.0 Universal:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The official NowRift logo remains a separate, unchanged branding asset.
Reproduction commands and output hashes are in `art/landing/README.md` and
`art/landing/manifest.json`.
