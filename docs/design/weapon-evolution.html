BOARD 01

Material, silhouette & restraint

Pixel contract

32 × 32 transparent cells; atlas 192 × 128. One clear pixel of transparent perimeter. Grip at (16,24); upright construction. Native 1× and review 4×; rig scale matches the existing 48px layered anatomy with 32px held art. No resampling, gradients, baked bloom or palette swaps in place of form changes.

Family identity

Forked heartwood and a suspended seed; split sunmetal and a luminous spine; faceted ice and a hovering crown; hooked volcanic metal and an open molten seam. Iron, wood, stone, leather and emissive highlights have distinct clusters.

Light & VFX

T0 plain. T1 material polish. T2 two-pixel glint. T3 controlled pulse. T4 two ambient pixels. T5 three ambient pixels and a brief attack arc. Low quality removes particles and trails. Reduced motion removes decorative animation.

Committed success

After the save commits: old silhouette → stepped forge pulse → new silhouette → tier label. 2.4 seconds total, Continue / Escape / close available immediately. Reduced motion reveals the new form without animation. Inventory and the held layer derive from the committed instance.

Failure

Real +5 outcome: +5, pity 1. No evolution reveal. Costs and chance remain governed by existing rules.

Downgrade

Real +12 failed outcome with a downgrade roll: +11, returning Mythic → Ascendant. No success animation. The canonical appearance follows the saved enhancement.

Reading order

Name → rarity → rank → form → next milestone → live probability → gold/materials → risk → confirmation. On mobile, comparison figures share one row within the existing scrolling detail panel.
